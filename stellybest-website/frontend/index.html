Who We Are

A Passion Project That
Became a Movement

STELLY-BEST VENTURES Int'l Ltd was born from a simple belief: Nigerian food deserves to be celebrated on the world stage. What started as a home kitchen experiment in Delta State has grown into an export-ready brand serving thousands of customers across Africa, Europe, North America, and beyond.

Our founder, a lifelong lover of Nigerian cuisine, saw how difficult it was for the diaspora to access authentic, quality plantain products. She set out to change that — sourcing directly from trusted farms, developing proprietary spice blends, and building a brand that Nigerians everywhere could be proud of.

Authenticity — every product tells a Nigerian story
Quality — no shortcuts, ever
Community — supporting local farmers and buyers alike
Ambition — building a global food brand from Africa

FAQ

Frequently Asked
Questions

Do you ship internationally? +
Yes! We ship to over 40 countries including the UK, USA, Canada, Australia, Germany, South Africa, and more. International shipping rates and timelines vary by destination.
Are your products NAFDAC certified? +
Yes. Our plantain chips are produced in NAFDAC-registered facilities and meet Nigerian food safety standards. Export documentation is available on request for wholesale buyers.
What are the chips made of? +
Our chips are made from 100% natural plantain with no artificial preservatives, colours, or flavours. The spiced version uses a proprietary West African spice blend.
How do I place a bulk or wholesale order? +
Visit our Bulk Orders page and fill out the enquiry form. We'll respond within 24 hours with pricing, minimum order quantities, and logistics options tailored to your needs.
How long does delivery take? +
Local delivery (Nigeria) typically takes 1–3 business days. International shipping ranges from 5–14 business days depending on the destination and shipping method chosen at checkout.
Can I order fresh plantain internationally? +
Fresh plantain export availability depends on destination country regulations. Please contact us directly and we'll confirm what's possible for your specific country.
Do you offer private label packaging? +
Yes, for qualified wholesale partners. We can package our products under your brand name. Contact us via the Bulk Orders form for details and minimum order requirements.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept bank transfers, Paystack, Flutterwave, and major international payment options. Secure payment gateway integration is in place to protect your transaction details.
